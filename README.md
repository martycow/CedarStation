# Cedar Station

This is my personal project. I want to make something unique, put a part of myself
into it, find my own voice as an author, and learn every side of making a game,
not just code.

## AI usage disclaimer

> **No AI-generated content.** Every model, texture, animation, sound, and line of text
> in the game is made by a human.

## What is it?

**Cedar Station** is a psychological thriller with stealth and puzzle mechanics,
in 3D low-poly with a PS2/Wii/Xbox 360-era look.

It tells the story of **Russell**, an anthropomorphic opossum who passes through
a small town in a region like the Pacific Northwest (inspired especially by Portland),
where he runs into frightening and mysterious events.
